## [Unreleased]

### Added

**The compute engine.** 251 wrapper modules across 30 category packages,
exposing 913 methods over one typed argument contract. Every method is a typed
stub that raises: the contract, the schemas and the gates are real, the bodies
are not written yet, and `engine.n_implemented` in `.github/inventory.json`
reports the honest figure by walking the syntax tree rather than by assertion.

**The contract and its artifacts.** Six committed JSON artifacts carry the node
specifications, the method cards, the decision trees, the recommendation
fixtures and the frozen argument-conformance corpus. Each is sealed with a
SHA-256 sidecar that `assert-inventory` verifies against an exact count. They
are inputs to the build, never outputs of it: the generated tier is re-emitted
from them in `--check` mode and compared byte for byte.

**The generated tier.** 64 machine-written modules — one argument model and one
documentation module per category, plus a manifest — regenerated by
`scripts/gen_schemas.py` and committed deliberately, so the diff of a contract
change is reviewable rather than invisible.

**Continuous integration.** Five workflows and an aggregate `ci-gate` that
refuses to pass unless every leaf reported success and the expected number of
leaves reported at all. Every gate asserts a minimum quantity of work done, so a
check that examined nothing cannot report success; several carry a planted
positive control that must fail on every run.

**The container.** A three-stage image whose build runs the full verification
suite as a hard gate, under a single-threaded numerical backend, as a non-root
user. An image that has not proven itself cannot exist.

**Governance.** AGPL-3.0-only throughout, a developer-certificate sign-off gate,
a branch ruleset requiring linear history and squash merges, and a repository
settings audit that fails on drift in the settings git cannot see.

### Known limitations

- No method computes yet. Every one of the 913 raises `NotImplementedError`,
  and the suite proves the argument surface only.
- The conformance harness is skipped until its oracle corpus is built, and the
  skip reason names exactly that.
- `backend/`, `frontend/` and `deploy/` are specified and not built; each says
  so in its own `README.md`.
